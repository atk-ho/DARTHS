Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 23, 2026

1. Information used

DARTHS may process player names, match scores, statistics, match history, game settings and preferences. Live sharing may also involve anonymous Firebase authentication identifiers and technical data handled by service providers.

2. Local storage

The application uses browser local storage to save players, matches and settings on your device. You can clear this data through the application or browser settings, but doing so may permanently remove your saved matches.

3. Online live matches

If you enable live sharing, game information is sent to Google Firebase Realtime Database so another device can view the scoreboard. Anyone with a valid match link may be able to view the shared match data, subject to database access rules.

4. Hosting and third parties

GitHub Pages hosts the website. Google Firebase provides optional online features. These providers may process technical information under their own policies. See Google Privacy Policy and GitHub Privacy Statement.

5. Sharing and sale

The application is not designed to sell personal information. Shared match data is visible to recipients of the match link, and technical data may be processed by hosting and Firebase providers.

6. Retention

Locally saved data remains until cleared. Online match data may remain in Firebase until removed by the application or administrator; no automatic deletion schedule is promised.

7. Security

Firebase authentication and database rules are intended to control online access, but no online service is completely secure. Do not enter sensitive personal information as player names.

8. Your choices

You can use basic scoring without enabling live sharing. You can export or clear locally saved data from the application Settings page.

9. Children

Users should avoid entering unnecessary personal information, especially information about children.

10. Questions and changes

This policy may be updated if the application changes. For questions or requests relating to online match data, contact the developer through GitHub Issues.
